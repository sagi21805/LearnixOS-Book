## Scope of Contributions
Currently, I am only accepting contributions for the "polishing" stage of the manuscript. Please limit your suggestions to:

* **Typos:** Spelling mistakes or punctuation errors.
* **Grammar:** Objective grammatical corrections.
* **Formatting:** Fixing broken Markdown links or layout issues.

**Note:** I am not looking for structural changes, new content, or plot/thematic alterations at this time.

## Policy on AI and LLMs
This book is a human-authored work. To preserve the unique style and "soul" of the writing:

1. **Human-Only Rewording:** Any suggestions for changes to wording or phrasing should be your own original thought.
2. **No LLMs:** Please do **not** use Large Language Models (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) to rewrite or suggest phrasing for this project and limit the use of them just to typo or grammar checking.
3. **Standard Tools:** The use of basic spell-checkers or standard dictionary tools is perfectly acceptable.

## How to Submit
1.  **Search:** Check the "Issues" tab to see if the error has already been reported.
2.  **Report:** If you find a typo, open an **Issue** or submit a **Pull Request**.
3.  **Be Specific:** Clearly state the current text, the suggested change, and the chapter/page where it was found.

Thank you for your help in refining this work!
